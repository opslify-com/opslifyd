Why this needs approving

Editing a guardrail is itself a Change — same diff, same approval, same signed trace. Nobody widens their own permissions quietly, and “who opened egress to that host, and when” is answerable a year later.

Narrowing — removing a host, adding a gate — applies without approval. Only widening is gated.

Precedence
Daemon default — the outer bound. Nothing below can exceed it.
Project policy — may only narrow.
Environment — prod narrows further.
Workspace file — untrusted; may narrow, never widen.

A repo that asks for more than the daemon allows is clamped, and the clamp is recorded.

Or from the shell
$ opslify policy allow-egress \ api.hetzner.cloud --env staging proposed change #133 — widening needs approval $ opslify policy show --env staging $ opslify policy diff staging prod $ opslify approve 133
